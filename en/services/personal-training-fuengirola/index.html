Main service

Personal training in Fuengirola built for steady progress.

This service is designed for people who want structure, not generic plans, and the kind of support that makes progress easier to sustain. Sessions are one to one, with full attention on technique, load, pacing and your real situation.

What the service includes

Assessment

Context and starting point

Before pushing hard, the process looks at what you want, what experience you bring and which real limits need to be respected.

Session

Useful technical work

Each session is built around exercise choice, rest and progression that match your goal, whether that is strength, recomposition or overall fitness.

Follow-up

Adjustments that matter

Progress is reviewed so loads, exercise choice or training frequency can change when recovery, schedule or response shifts.

The service is built so you understand why you are doing what you are doing. The point is not simply to train hard, but to stack enough well-directed weeks together.

That difference matters most for beginners, for people rebuilding consistency and for people who already train but no longer move forward. Personalisation also means avoiding unnecessary complexity.

Real progress often appears when a plan stops being chaotic and becomes repeatable. That is why every session sits inside a simple progression with clear priorities and regular review.

Common goals

  • Improve strength and movement quality.
  • Lose fat through a sustainable process.
  • Rebuild routine after a long break.
  • Train with more confidence and less guesswork.

How to start

There is no complicated intake process. The fastest start is simply to explain your goal on WhatsApp and check availability.

  1. You message on WhatsApp with your main goal.
  2. Availability is checked and basic questions are answered.
  3. A first session is booked with a clear starting direction.
