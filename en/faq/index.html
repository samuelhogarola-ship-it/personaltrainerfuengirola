Common questions

Frequently asked questions about personal training in Fuengirola.

This page gives direct answers to the most common questions before booking: level, price, first session, training frequency, languages and contact.

Do I need previous gym experience to train with Sorvali?

No. The service is prepared both for complete beginners and for people who already train but want more structure and better progress.

How much does a personal training session in Fuengirola cost?

A one-to-one session costs €50. The price is shown openly on the website to remove basic friction before you message.

What is covered in the first session?

The first session reviews your main goal, previous experience, real availability and the smartest way to begin without improvising.

How many times per week should I train?

That depends on your level, goal and real-life schedule. Frequency is adapted so the process stays sustainable.

Can I train if I speak English or Finnish?

Yes. The site and acquisition flow are prepared for Spanish, English and Finnish, which is especially useful for residents and long stays on the Costa del Sol.

Can this service help with fat loss or body recomposition?

Yes. A large part of the work is aimed at body recomposition, general physical improvement and rebuilding useful habits.

How do I book a session?

Booking and availability checks happen through WhatsApp. It is the quickest channel for questions and a first session.

Why are there no testimonials on the website yet?

This version prioritises clear and verifiable information. If testimonials are added later, they will be shown with real context and no generic filler.

If your question is not here, WhatsApp is the fastest next step

A short message explaining your goal is usually enough to clarify whether the service fits and how to begin.
