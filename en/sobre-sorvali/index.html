About Sorvali

A direct, structured way to train in Fuengirola.

Sorvali works with people who want real results without generic routines. The method starts with strength and conditioning, then adapts to your level, history, availability and main goal.

How she works

Every session has a purpose: better technique, appropriate load, clear progression and enough context so you understand what you are doing. The goal is not to impress you with complexity; it is to make training work in real life.

What you can expect

Individual attention, technical correction, honest feedback and a plan that respects your starting point. The process is practical, serious and easy to follow.

A first conversation

The best way to know if the service fits you is to explain your goal and your current situation. From there, Sorvali can recommend a first session or answer questions before booking.
