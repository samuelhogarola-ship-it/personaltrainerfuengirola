FAQ

Clear answers before you book.

Common questions about level, pricing, the first session, frequency and how to contact Sorvali.

Do I need previous experience?

No. The service works for beginners and for people who already train but want more structure.

How much does a session cost?

A single session costs 60 EUR. The 10-session pack costs 550 EUR and includes a basic weekly routine.

What happens in the first session?

Your goal, experience, availability and starting point are reviewed so the first step is not improvised.

Where are the sessions?

The service is local to Fuengirola. Availability, location details and the best format are confirmed directly before booking.

Which languages can I use?

You can use Spanish, English or Finnish when contacting Sorvali and reading the website.

How do I book?

Booking and availability are handled by WhatsApp. It is the fastest way to ask questions and arrange a first session.
