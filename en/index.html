Personal training in Fuengirola for sustainable progress

The difference between training on impulse and training with direction usually comes down to three things: a proper starting assessment, realistic progression and someone keeping the process grounded when motivation changes.

That is Sorvali's role as a personal trainer in Fuengirola. The goal is not to exhaust you once, but to build a process you can keep repeating week after week.

If you want a trainer who combines local context, structure and technical judgement, this service is built for residents, long stays and especially Finnish clients who want to understand where to train and how to start without guesswork.

The service is not just about training hard. It is about training better. The method is built around assessment, technique, progression and ongoing review.
