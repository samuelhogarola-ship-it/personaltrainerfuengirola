Online does not mean generic

A useful online routine is not a table sent with no context. It needs a goal, clear exercises, frequency, notes and a way to review progress. If you search personal trainer Fuengirola but travel, work changing hours or prefer starting from home, this format can fit.

What you need for it to work

You need honesty about time, available equipment and technical level. If you have never trained, it may be better to combine a first in-person session with online follow-up. If you already have a base, online routine can give direction.

The goal is not filling the week with tasks, but choosing what matters and making it repeatable.
