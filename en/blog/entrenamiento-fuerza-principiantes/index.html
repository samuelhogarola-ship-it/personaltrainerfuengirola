Strength

How to start strength training without overcomplicating it.

A beginner-friendly guide to build a base, avoid common mistakes and progress without anxiety.

Start simple

The first goal is not to do everything. It is to learn the basic movements, repeat them well and create a realistic rhythm.

Avoid changing everything every week

Constantly switching routines feels busy, but it often prevents progress. A simple structure repeated consistently works better.

Technique first

Receiving correction early helps avoid bad habits and makes the whole process more efficient.
