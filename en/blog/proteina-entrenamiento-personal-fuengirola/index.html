Protein and personal training in Fuengirola: no-confusion guide

Published: 6 July 2026 Author: Sorvali Category: Sports nutrition

Why protein gets so much attention

Protein appears in almost every conversation about strength, fat loss and body recomposition. It makes sense: it supports tissue repair, muscle maintenance and recovery for the next session. In personal trainer Fuengirola work, the useful question is not only how much protein to eat, but how to make it sustainable.

Many people know they should eat more protein, but fail because there is no plan. If there are no easy options at the end of the day, improvisation wins.

Simple beats perfect

Eggs, fish, chicken, yogurt, legumes, tofu or fresh cheese can all work depending on preference. You do not need to eat the same thing every day. You need two or three quick solutions for busy, hot or low-energy days.

A personal trainer Fuengirola does not replace clinical nutrition advice, but can help you see whether your basic routine supports training. If recovery, food or energy are poor, progress suffers.

Supplements only if they help

A shake can be practical, but it is not mandatory. If it helps you reach a reasonable base without stress, it can make sense. If it hides chaotic eating, the problem remains.

The idea is simple: protein in main meals, enough water and a routine you can repeat in Fuengirola, Malaga or when travelling.
