What to eat before and after training in Fuengirola

Published: 10 July 2026 Author: Sorvali Category: Sports nutrition

There is no single perfect meal

What to eat before training depends on timing, heat, digestion and session type. If you train with a personal trainer Fuengirola in the morning, something light may be enough. If you train later, a normal meal a few hours before can work.

The important thing is arriving with energy and without heaviness. Basic sports nutrition starts by noticing what works for you, not copying absolute rules.

Before training

A simple combination of easy carbohydrates and some protein can work: yogurt with fruit, toast with eggs, rice with fish or a normal meal if there is time. The closer the session, the lighter it usually needs to be.

If you train in Fuengirola heat, add hydration. Sometimes the problem is not food but arriving with too little water.

After training

After training, you do not need to run to eat within ten minutes. The useful thing is that the next meal includes protein, some carbohydrate when recovery needs it and foods you can repeat.

A personal trainer Fuengirola can help identify whether energy dips come from poor pre-training food, low sleep or too much training load. Everything matters, but not everything is solved by more food.
