Professional profile

A personal trainer focused on clarity, technique and consistency.

Sorvali works with people who want real results in Fuengirola without generic plans. The approach starts from strength and conditioning, but it is always adapted to the client's current level, history, schedule and main goal.

Sorvali presents herself as a certified personal trainer with a focus on strength, conditioning and body transformation. In practice, that means a very specific way of working: assess first, simplify second and build a process that a real person can keep going over time.

Instead of promising flashy workouts or quick results, the priority is useful progression. That means choosing exercises well, adjusting load to the actual level of the client and giving technique enough attention so training builds confidence rather than frustration.

The relationship with the client is part of the method too. Some people look for a personal trainer in Fuengirola because they have never managed to stay consistent. Others already train but feel stuck. In both cases, the work is about guidance with judgement, not just assigning exercises.

What the work is built on

  • Strength as a base for physical progress and better function.
  • Real adaptation to your starting point and available time.
  • Technical coaching during one-to-one sessions.
  • Ongoing follow-up when life or recovery changes.
  • Direct WhatsApp communication and simple booking.

What someone can expect when training with Sorvali

A session with purpose

Every session belongs to a bigger process. The training is organised around a clear goal, not random exercise variety.

A close working style

Personalisation also means listening. Schedule, energy, experience and daily life all matter when building progression.

A technical framework

The promise is not to train harder just for the sake of it, but to train better so progress becomes more consistent.

If you want to see whether the fit is right, the best step is to talk

The quickest way to know whether this service suits you is to explain your goal and starting point. From there, it is easy to recommend a first session or answer questions before booking.
